Case study: one GKE cluster

The cluster ran on three large nodes at 11 to 14% CPU. Pods reserved 16.1 cores and used about 3.2, and about 25 of them, the ingress included, had no resource requests at all. Kubecost put a third of the node spend down to idle capacity.

The goal was to cut cost as far as possible while keeping room to autoscale at peak, with no production outage during the move.

  1. Set requests for about 30 apps from 11 days of real usage: CPU at two to three times the average, memory at the peak plus 15%.
  2. Added a disruption budget to every workload, going from 3 to 63, so draining a node could never take down every copy of a service.
  3. Replaced the fixed pool with autoscaling pools: a steady on-demand pool, plus a Spot pool that falls back to on-demand when Spot runs out.
  4. Kept customer-facing apps off Spot. A Spot reclaim gives about 30 seconds of warning, and those apps take three minutes to become ready.
  5. Moved the apps over one group at a time, drained the old nodes against the budgets, and deleted the old pool.
